What is Paper Trading?

The pros, the cons, and the "psychology gap" between trading with fake money and real money.

The Concept

Paper trading is the practice of making hypothetical buy and sell decisions using a simulated account with fake money. The name comes from an era before computers when traders literally tracked trades on paper.

Bottom Line: Paper trading is your sandbox. It's where you learn, experiment, and make expensive mistakes — for free.

✅ Advantages

  • Zero financial risk — learn from mistakes for free
  • Test strategies before committing real capital
  • Master the platform without pressure
  • Build a track record and confidence
  • Blow up? Reset and try again

⚠️ Limitations

  • No emotional stakes — fake money feels different
  • No slippage — paper trades execute at exact prices
  • Over-confidence risk — paper profits ≠ real profits
  • You hold losers longer on paper (no real pain)
  • No market impact from large orders

The Psychology Gap

The biggest challenge when transitioning from paper to real trading.

Paper traders act 3× faster. Real traders hesitate due to fear.

83% of paper traders honor stops. Only 41% of real traders do.

Average 15–25% win rate drop when switching to real trading.

Bridge the Gap: Treat your paper account like it's real. Honor every stop-loss and size positions as if real capital is at risk.
