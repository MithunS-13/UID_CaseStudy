The Four Core Order Types

Every order you'll ever place falls into one of these categories

⚡
INSTANT

Market Order

A market order executes immediately at the best available price. You get filled instantly — but you don't control the exact price you pay.

You set:Quantity only
Guaranteed fill?Yes
Guaranteed price?No (slippage risk)
Use when: You need to enter or exit a position immediately, regardless of the exact price. Ideal for highly liquid assets.
🎯
PRICE-SPECIFIC

Limit Order

A limit order only executes at your specified price OR better. You control the exact price, but there's no guarantee of a fill if the price never reaches your level.

You set:Quantity + Max price
Guaranteed fill?Only if price reached
Guaranteed price?Yes (or better)
Use when: You want to buy a dip or sell a rally at a specific price. Patient traders use limit orders to get better fills.
🛡️
PROTECTION

Stop-Loss Order

A stop-loss is a pre-set instruction to exit a trade if it moves against you by a specified amount. It's the most important risk management tool available to traders.

Trigger:When price drops to stop level
Converts to:Market order when triggered
Max loss:Pre-defined
NEVER trade without one. A stop-loss is your financial airbag. It limits your loss to an acceptable amount if the trade goes wrong.
💰
PROFIT

Take-Profit Order

A take-profit order automatically closes your position when the price reaches your target. It locks in your gains without requiring you to monitor the screen 24/7.

Trigger:When price rises to target
Converts to:Limit order (usually)
Benefit:Guaranteed profit-taking
Use when: You have a specific profit target. Set it when you enter a trade, so emotions don't prevent you from taking profits when they're available.
Bracket Order — The Professional's Setup

A bracket order combines three orders simultaneously: your entry + stop-loss + take-profit. It creates a "bracket" of protection around your position.

TP
Take-Profit: $215.00
+$15.00 (+7.5%) above entry
E
Entry: $200.00
Market order executed at this price
SL
Stop-Loss: $190.00
-$10.00 (-5%) below entry
Risk/Reward Ratio: 1:1.5
Risking $10 to make $15. Always aim for at least 1:2 (risk $1 to make $2) for long-term profitability.
