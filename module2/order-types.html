Order Types Explained

Visualizing Market, Limit, Stop-Loss, and Take-Profit orders — and when to use each one.

⚡ Market Order

Executes immediately at the best available price. Guaranteed fill, but you don't control the exact price. Subject to slippage.

🎯 Limit Order

Only executes at your specified price or better. Guarantees price but not fill. May not execute if price never reaches your level.

🛡️ Stop-Loss

Exits a trade if it moves against you by a specified amount. Converts to a market order when triggered. NEVER trade without one.

💰 Take-Profit

Closes your position when price reaches your target. Locks in gains automatically without requiring you to monitor the screen.
