AAPL Long Trade — Step Walkthrough

The 7-Step Trading Process

1
Market Analysis

Identify the trend and find a setup that gives you a reason to trade — your "thesis."

2
Define Risk (Stop-Loss)

Decide where you're WRONG before thinking about profits. Set your stop at a logical technical level.

3
Calculate Position Size

1% Rule: max risk = $1,000. If stop is $5 away → max 200 shares.

4
Set Take-Profit Target

Aim for at least 2:1 reward-to-risk. Place target at next key resistance level.

5
Place the Bracket Order

Enter trade + stop-loss + take-profit simultaneously. You're now fully protected.

6
Monitor & Manage

Watch for changes. Trail your stop to lock in profits as price moves in your favor.

7
Close & Review

Trade exits via TP, SL, or manual close. Record the trade in your journal.
