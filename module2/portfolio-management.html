Key Portfolio Concepts

Understanding these terms is essential for managing your paper trading account

💵 Buying Power

The amount of capital you have available to open new positions. Decreases when you buy; increases when you sell.

Current Buying Power
$42,160 available 39% of $107K

📊 Margin

Borrowed money from your broker that amplifies both gains AND losses. A 2:1 margin means you can control $200K with $100K. Dangerous for beginners.

Warning: In paper trading, avoid margin until you understand leverage. Many traders lose accounts by over-leveraging.

📈 Total Equity

Your total account value = Cash + Market value of all open positions. This is the number that represents your true wealth in the account.

Equity Formula:
Cash + Σ(Qty × Current Price)
= $42,160 + $65,180
= $107,340

💹 Position Sizing

How much of your capital to allocate to a single trade. The golden rule: never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single position.

1% Risk Rule on $100K account:
Max risk per trade = $1,000
If stop is $5 away → max 200 shares

⚖️ Diversification

Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Spreading capital across uncorrelated assets reduces the impact of any single trade going wrong.

🎯 Win Rate vs R:R

A trader with a 40% win rate can be VERY profitable if their wins are 3× bigger than their losses. Track both metrics.
