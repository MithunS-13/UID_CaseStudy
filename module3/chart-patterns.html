Geometric Chart Patterns

Triangles, Wedges, and Head-and-Shoulders — recurring formations that signal major price moves.

Triangle Patterns

Symmetrical Triangle

Both lines converge equally. Neutral — wait for the breakout before trading.

Ascending Triangle

Flat top + rising lows. Bullish — usually breaks upward.

Descending Triangle

Flat bottom + falling highs. Bearish — usually breaks downward.

Wedge Patterns

Rising Wedge (Bearish Reversal)

Price rises but in a narrowing channel. Despite the upward move, this is a bearish reversal pattern.

Falling Wedge (Bullish Reversal)

Price falls in a narrowing channel. Despite the downward move, this is a bullish reversal pattern.

Head & Shoulders

Three peaks: left shoulder, a higher head, and right shoulder at about the same level as the left.

Step 1: Price rises to left shoulder peak, then pulls back
Step 2: Price rallies higher to form the head, then pulls back
Step 3: Price rises again to form right shoulder (lower than head)
Step 4: Break below neckline = confirmed sell signal

Flags & Pennants

Continuation patterns that appear after a strong move (the "flagpole"). Brief pause before the trend continues.

Bull Flag

Strong up move (flagpole) + slight downward channel (flag). Breaks upward to continue trend.

Pennant

Like a flag but the consolidation is a converging triangle. Also a continuation pattern.
