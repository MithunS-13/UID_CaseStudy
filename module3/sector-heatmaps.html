What is Sector Rotation?

The flow of money from one industry sector to another as investors react to changes in the economic cycle.

Key Insight: No sector stays hot forever. When technology is overvalued, money rotates into undervalued sectors like energy or utilities.
Recovery: Financials, Technology, Consumer Discretionary lead
Expansion: Industrials, Materials, Energy lead
Peak: Energy remains strong. Healthcare begins outperforming.
Recession: Utilities, Healthcare, Staples (defensive) hold up best.
