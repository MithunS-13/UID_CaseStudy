What is Sector Rotation?

Sector rotation is the flow of money from one industry sector to another as investors react to changes in the economic cycle. Understanding this flow is how macro traders identify opportunities before the crowd.

Key Insight: No sector stays hot forever. When technology is overvalued, money rotates into undervalued sectors like energy or utilities. Heatmaps reveal this rotation in real-time.

The Business Cycle & Sector Rotation

Recovery Phase

Financials, Technology, Consumer Discretionary outperform as growth expectations rise.

Expansion Phase

Industrials, Materials, Energy lead as the economy grows and commodities surge.

Peak Phase

Energy and Basic Materials remain strong. Healthcare begins to outperform.

Recession Phase

Utilities, Healthcare, Consumer Staples (defensive sectors) hold up best as growth slows.

How to Use Heatmaps in Your Trading

Find the Leaders

The darkest green sectors are where momentum is strongest. These are the sectors to focus your longs on.

Avoid the Laggards

Red sectors are seeing distribution. Don't fight the trend — avoid buying into sectors with persistent red heatmaps.

Watch the Rotation

When a previously green sector turns red AND a previously red sector turns green, that's a rotation. Early identification = early entry.
