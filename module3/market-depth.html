Market Depth & Level 2

Visualizing the Order Book — the real-time list of buy and sell orders that determines where price will go next.

The Order Book (Level 2)

Shows all pending buy (bid) and sell (ask) orders at every price level. This is the raw supply and demand visible in real-time before trades execute.

Level 1 = Best bid + Best ask only
Level 2 = ALL pending orders at ALL price levels
Bid Side (Buyers) — Orders to buy below current price. Large bids = strong support.
Ask Side (Sellers) — Orders to sell above current price. Large asks = strong resistance.
Bid-Ask Spread — Gap between best bid and best ask. Tight spread = liquid market.
