Correlation Matrix

Visualizing how different assets move together or apart — the foundation of diversification strategy.

What is Correlation?

Correlation measures how consistently two assets move in the same direction. Expressed as a value between -1 and +1.

+1.0 = Perfect Positive+1.0

When one goes up 1%, the other ALWAYS goes up 1%.

0.0 = No Correlation0.0

The two assets move completely independently.

-1.0 = Perfect Negative-1.0

When one rises, the other ALWAYS falls. A perfect hedge.

Why it matters: Holding highly correlated assets gives you the ILLUSION of diversification. AAPL and MSFT both drop when tech sells off.
