Trendlines & Channels

How to draw, validate, and trade support and resistance trendlines — the most fundamental skill in technical analysis.

Rules for Valid Trendlines

Rule 1: Minimum 2 touch points. The 3rd touch CONFIRMS the line.
Rule 2: Connect actual price pivots — never force the angle.
Rule 3: More touches = more valid. 5-touch line > 2-touch line.
Rule 4: Breaks are significant. Old support often becomes new resistance.

Support & Resistance

Support: Price level where buying demand prevents further decline. Price "bounces" off this floor.

Resistance: Price level where selling pressure halts a rally. Price "reverses" from this ceiling.
Support → Resistance

When price breaks through support, that level often flips to become resistance.

Channel Types

Ascending Channel

Higher highs & lows. Buy lower line, target upper line.

Descending Channel

Lower highs & lows. Bearish bias. Short at upper line.

Horizontal Channel

Sideways range. Buy support, sell resistance.
