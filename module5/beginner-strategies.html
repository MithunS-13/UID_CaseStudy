Top 5 Beginner Strategies

Visual walkthroughs of the simplest, most reliable setups to help you build a positive track record before trading with real money.

Before You Begin: These strategies are starting points — not guarantees. Every market is different, every asset has unique characteristics, and no strategy works 100% of the time. The goal is to give you a testable framework, not a "holy grail."
1
Most Popular

Moving Average Crossover

MA Crossover — Golden & Death Crosses

The simplest trend-following strategy. When a fast moving average crosses above a slow one (golden cross), buy. When it crosses below (death cross), sell or go short.

Entry Signal (BUY)

20-day SMA crosses ABOVE the 50-day SMA. Enter at the close of the crossover candle.

Exit Signal (SELL)

20-day SMA crosses BELOW the 50-day SMA. Exit the position at the close.

Stop-Loss

Place stop below the most recent swing low or below the 50-day SMA.

Best timeframe: Daily charts. Use weekly charts to confirm the overall trend direction first.
2
High Reliability

Support & Resistance Bounce

Buy when price pulls back to a well-established support level in an uptrend. Sell at resistance. This strategy leverages the predictable behavior of key price levels.

Setup Conditions
  • Price is in a clear uptrend (higher highs and lows)
  • A key support level has been tested 2+ times
  • Price pulls back to touch that support level again
Entry

Buy when price touches support AND shows a bullish candlestick pattern (e.g., hammer, engulfing). Don't buy the moment price touches support — wait for confirmation.

Stop-Loss

Below the support level by a small margin (ATR × 0.5). If price breaks support, your analysis is wrong.

3
Momentum Based

RSI Oversold Bounce

RSI Oversold → Bullish Bounce

Buy when the RSI drops below 30 (oversold) and then crosses back ABOVE 30. This signals that selling momentum has exhausted and a bounce is likely.

Filter: Only take trades WITH the trend

Only buy RSI oversold signals when the overall daily/weekly trend is UP. Don't try to buy oversold in a strong downtrend — it's catching a falling knife.

Entry

RSI crosses back above 30 on a 4-hour or daily chart, while the weekly trend is bullish.

Target

Exit when RSI reaches 70 (overbought) or at the next key resistance level.

4
Volume Confirmed

Volume Breakout

Buy when price breaks above a key resistance level on significantly higher-than-average volume. Volume confirms the breakout is real and not a "fakeout."

What counts as "high volume"?

At least 2× the 20-day average volume on the breakout candle. Some traders require 3× for maximum conviction.

Entry

Buy at the close of the breakout candle, or on a "retest" of the broken resistance level (which now acts as support).

The Fakeout Risk

Breakouts that occur on low volume frequently reverse. Always wait for volume confirmation before entering.

5
Trend Following

Pullback to Moving Average

Pullback to 20-Day SMA

In a strong uptrend, price will frequently pull back to test the 20-day or 50-day SMA before resuming higher. These pullbacks offer low-risk entries with the trend.

Setup Conditions
  • Price above the 50 SMA (uptrend confirmed)
  • Price pulls back to touch the 20 SMA
  • Appears in 3-5 candles of consolidation
Entry

Buy when price closes back above the 20 SMA after touching it. The MA acts as a "springboard."

Why this works: Trend traders who missed the initial move see the pullback as their chance to enter. Their buying pushes price higher.
