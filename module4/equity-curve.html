Reading Your Equity Curve

The equity curve is the visual autobiography of your trading system. Its shape tells you everything about your strategy's health.

Ideal: Smooth, Rising — Consistent, robust strategy. Gently sloping upward.
Warning: Volatile, Jagged — Poor risk management. Psychologically devastating.
Flat Periods = Drawdowns — Every strategy has them. Focus on depth and duration.
Always Compare to Benchmark — If you barely beat the S&P 500, why take the risk?
