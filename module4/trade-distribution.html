Reading the Histogram

  • Left Tail — Big losers. A long left tail = risk management problem.
  • Right Tail — Big winners. Long right tail = great for expectancy.
  • Spike at -1R — Ideally your losses cluster near -1R (stops honored).
Healthy Distribution:
✓ Right tail extends further than left
✓ Most losses cluster near -1R
✓ Significant portion of wins are 2R+
Warning Signs:
✗ Left tail extends far (letting losers run)
✗ All wins are tiny (1:1 or less R:R)
