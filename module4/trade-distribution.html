What Your Histogram Reveals

A histogram of your trade P&L is like an X-ray of your trading psychology and system quality. Here's what the different shapes mean:

Healthy: Wins skewed right, losses tight left

Your winning trades extend far to the right (big wins) while your losing trades cluster tightly to the left of zero (small, controlled losses). This is the pattern of a disciplined trader.

Warning: Fat left tail (large losses)

Large losses scattered far left of zero indicate you're not respecting stop-losses. You're holding losers too long, hoping they recover. This will eventually destroy an account.

Warning: Thin right tail (cutting wins short)

All your wins cluster close to zero — you're taking profits too early. You're letting fear cut your winners short. This is "cutting flowers and watering weeds."

Bimodal distribution

Two separate peaks in the histogram often indicate you have two distinct strategies or market regimes in your data. This is worth investigating to understand when each works.

Win/Loss Streak Analysis

Understanding how your wins and losses are distributed sequentially — not just in aggregate — helps manage the psychological pressure of trading.

The Gambler's Fallacy: After 5 losing trades, the next trade is NOT more likely to win. Each trade is independent. Understanding this prevents the dangerous behavior of "doubling down" to recover losses.
