Understanding R-Multiples

An "R-multiple" expresses every trade as a ratio of how much you made vs your initial risk. Normalizes all trades to the same scale.

R-Multiple Formula:
Risk $200 and make $400 → that's a +2R trade
Risk $200 and lose $200 → that's a -1R trade
+3R trade — Made 3× your initial risk. Keeps system profitable even with 40% win rate.
-1R trade — Lost exactly your initial risk. What your stop-loss ensures.
