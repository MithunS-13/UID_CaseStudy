Understanding Drawdowns

A drawdown is the decline from a peak in your account equity to a subsequent trough — before a new peak is established.

The Math of Recovery:
25% loss → need 33% gain to recover
50% loss → need 100% gain to recover
75% loss → need 300% gain to recover
Protect capital first.
  • Max Drawdown — Largest peak-to-trough decline over a period
  • Duration — How long the account was below its peak
  • Recovery Time — Time from trough back to a new peak
