A deep dive into OHLC data — the four numbers that define every bar on every chart in the world.

The OHLC Structure

Every candlestick encodes exactly four pieces of information for a given time period. Hover over each part of the candlestick to learn what it represents.

Open (O)The first traded price when the candle period began. Forms the top or bottom of the body.
High (H)The highest price traded during the period. Forms the tip of the upper wick (shadow).
Low (L)The lowest price traded during the period. Forms the tip of the lower wick.
Close (C)The last traded price when the candle period ended. The most important price in the candle.
Key Rule: If Close > Open → Bullish candle (green). If Close < Open → Bearish candle (red).
