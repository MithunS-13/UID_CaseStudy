The Anatomy of a Candlestick

A deep dive into OHLC data — the four numbers that define every bar on every chart in the world.

The OHLC Structure

Every candlestick encodes exactly four pieces of information. Hover each label to highlight the matching part of the candle.

Open (O)

The first traded price when the candle period began.

High (H)

The highest price traded. Forms the tip of the upper wick.

Low (L)

The lowest price traded. Forms the tip of the lower wick.

Close (C)

The last traded price. The most important of the four values.

Key Rule: If Close > Open → Bullish candle (green). If Close < Open → Bearish candle (red).

Bullish vs Bearish Candles

🟢 Bullish (Green)

Close is higher than Open. Buyers won. Longer body = stronger buying pressure.

🔴 Bearish (Red)

Close is lower than Open. Sellers won. Longer body = stronger selling pressure.
