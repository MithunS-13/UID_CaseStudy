Technical Indicators 101

Overlay Moving Averages, Bollinger Bands, and the Ichimoku Cloud on price charts to reveal momentum, volatility, and trend strength.

Moving Averages (MA)

A Moving Average smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price. It removes short-term fluctuations to reveal the underlying trend direction.

SMA 20 (Fast) — Average of last 20 closing prices. Sensitive to recent moves.
SMA 50 (Slow) — Average of last 50 prices. Captures the medium-term trend.
SMA 200 (Trend) — The "golden" baseline. Above = bull market; below = bear market.
Golden Cross: When SMA 20 crosses above SMA 50 → bullish signal.
Death Cross: When SMA 20 crosses below SMA 50 → bearish signal.

SMA vs EMA: What's the Difference?

Simple Moving Average (SMA)

Treats all periods equally. Slower to react to new data. Better for identifying long-term trends. Less prone to "whipsaws."

Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

Gives more weight to recent prices. Faster to react. Generates earlier signals. But also generates more false signals.

Bollinger Bands

Created by John Bollinger, Bollinger Bands consist of a middle SMA (usually 20-period) plus two "bands" plotted 2 standard deviations above and below. They measure volatility dynamically.

Upper Band

SMA(20) + 2σ. When price tags the upper band in a strong trend, it's a sign of STRENGTH, not weakness.

Middle Band

The 20-period SMA. Acts as support in uptrends and resistance in downtrends.

Lower Band

SMA(20) − 2σ. Price touching the lower band can signal oversold conditions.

The Squeeze: When the bands contract (narrow), it signals low volatility and a BIG move is coming. When they expand rapidly, volatility has returned.

Ichimoku Cloud

Developed in Japan in the 1960s, the Ichimoku Cloud is a comprehensive indicator that shows trend, momentum, support/resistance, and future price projections — all in one chart.

Tenkan-sen (Conversion)

(9-high + 9-low) / 2. Fast trend line. Like a 9-period MA of the midpoint.

Kijun-sen (Base)

(26-high + 26-low) / 2. Slow trend line. Key support/resistance.

Senkou Span A (Cloud edge)

(Tenkan + Kijun) / 2, plotted 26 periods AHEAD.

Senkou Span B (Cloud edge)

(52-high + 52-low) / 2, plotted 26 periods ahead.

Chikou Span (Lagging)

Current close plotted 26 periods BEHIND. Confirms signal direction.

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI is a momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of price changes. It oscillates between 0 and 100, revealing overbought and oversold conditions.

Above 70 = Overbought

Asset has rallied too fast. Potential reversal or pullback zone. Consider taking profits.

40–60 = Neutral Zone

Neither overbought nor oversold. Wait for the RSI to reach an extreme before acting.

Below 30 = Oversold

Asset has sold off too aggressively. Potential bounce or reversal. Look for buying opportunities.

RSI Divergence: Price makes a new high but RSI makes a lower high = bearish divergence. A powerful reversal signal.
