Technical Indicators 101

Moving Averages, Bollinger Bands, and RSI — the three most popular indicators explained with visual examples.

Moving Averages (MA)

A Moving Average smooths price data by averaging closing prices over a set period. It removes short-term noise to reveal the underlying trend direction.

SMA 20 (Fast) — Average of last 20 prices. Sensitive to recent moves.
SMA 50 (Slow) — Average of last 50 prices. Captures medium-term trend.
Golden Cross: SMA 20 crosses above SMA 50 → buy signal.
Death Cross: SMA 20 crosses below SMA 50 → sell signal.

Bollinger Bands

A 20-period SMA with upper and lower bands set at 2 standard deviations. They measure volatility dynamically — bands widen when volatile, contract when calm.

Upper Band — SMA(20) + 2σ. Tagging the upper band in a strong trend shows strength.
Middle Band — The 20-period SMA. Support in uptrends, resistance in downtrends.
Lower Band — SMA(20) − 2σ. Can signal oversold conditions.
The Squeeze: When bands contract tightly, a big move is coming.

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

A momentum oscillator between 0 and 100. Measures the speed and magnitude of price changes, revealing overbought and oversold conditions.

Above 70 = Overbought — Asset rallied too fast. Potential reversal zone.
40–60 = Neutral — Wait for extremes before acting.
Below 30 = Oversold — Potential bounce. Look for buy opportunities.
RSI Divergence: Price makes new high but RSI makes lower high = bearish divergence. Powerful reversal signal.

Ichimoku Cloud

Developed in Japan in the 1960s. Shows trend, momentum, and support/resistance all in one chart using five lines and a "cloud" area.

Tenkan-sen (9) — Fast line. (9-high + 9-low) / 2
Kijun-sen (26) — Slow line. Key support/resistance.
Senkou Span A & B — Form the "cloud" (Kumo).
Key Rule: Price above green cloud = uptrend. Price below red cloud = downtrend.
