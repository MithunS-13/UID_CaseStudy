The Power of Volume

Volume is the fuel behind price movements. Learn to read volume bars and Volume Profile to understand where the real money is moving.

Volume: The Missing Dimension

Price tells you where the market went. Volume tells you how much conviction was behind that move. A 5% rally on 10 million shares means something very different from a 5% rally on 100 million shares.

The Golden Rule of Volume: "Volume precedes price." Unusual volume spikes often appear BEFORE the major price move — making volume one of the most predictive indicators.
📈
Rising Price + High Volume
Strong, confirmed uptrend. The move has conviction.
📈
Rising Price + Low Volume
Weak rally. Likely to reverse. Be cautious — no participation.
📉
Falling Price + High Volume
Panic selling. Distribution or capitulation. Key reversal zone.
📉
Falling Price + Low Volume
Pullback within an uptrend. Healthy consolidation.

Key Volume Profile Concepts

Point of Control (POC)

The price level where the MOST volume was traded. Often acts as a magnet — price frequently returns to test it. The most important level on any Volume Profile.

Value Area (VA)

The range where 70% of all volume occurred. Price inside the value area is considered "fair." Price outside the value area is considered "extreme."

Low Volume Nodes (LVN)

Price levels where very little trading happened. Price tends to move THROUGH these areas quickly, like a hot knife through butter.

High Volume Nodes (HVN)

Heavy trading zones that act as support or resistance. Price often consolidates or reverses at these levels.

On-Balance Volume (OBV)
A cumulative indicator that adds volume on up days and subtracts on down days
How to read OBV: When OBV rises while price is flat or falling, it signals accumulation — big buyers absorbing shares quietly. This often precedes a breakout.
