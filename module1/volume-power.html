The Power of Volume

Volume is the fuel behind price movements. Learn to read volume bars and understand where the real money is moving.

Volume: The Missing Dimension

Price tells you where the market went. Volume tells you how much conviction was behind that move.

The Golden Rule: "Volume precedes price." Unusual volume spikes often appear BEFORE the major price move.
Rising Price + High Volume — Strong uptrend with conviction
Rising Price + Low Volume — Weak rally, likely to reverse
Falling Price + High Volume — Panic selling, key reversal zone
Falling Price + Low Volume — Healthy pullback in an uptrend

Price + Volume Chart:

Volume Profile

Instead of volume over time, Volume Profile shows where volume occurred at each PRICE LEVEL. This reveals key support and resistance.

Point of Control (POC)

The price level where the MOST volume was traded. Price frequently returns to test it.

Value Area (VA)

The range where 70% of all volume occurred. Inside = "fair price." Outside = "extreme."

Low Volume Nodes (LVN)

Price levels where very little trading happened. Price moves through these quickly.
